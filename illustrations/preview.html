Usage: inline SVG (React/HTML/JS) inherits color from any ancestor. Optional tokens: --nexa-ill-fg, --nexa-ill-soft, --nexa-ill-accent. Sizes are scalable — 48/96/192 shown; the 64×64 viewBox stays crisp at every size.

Illustrations ship decorative: add aria-hidden="true" when they sit next to a label, or role="img" + <title>/aria-label when they are the only content. Full usage and the CSS-mask recipe are in README.md. Manifest: manifest.json.
